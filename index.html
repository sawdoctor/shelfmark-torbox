Shelfmark audiobooks

A personal audiobook installation using rclone to access its owner's Google Drive library and backup vault.

Google authorization lets the owner's server list audiobook files, read their metadata and audio for playback, and, where separately configured and authorized, write audiobook backups to the owner's backup vault. TorBox playback is managed separately.

This installation is for the owner's personal use. Family playback does not give family members access to the Google account or its credentials. It is not a public Google sign-in service.

This information website does not request Google authorization, receive OAuth credentials or serve audiobook files.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use.

Project and maintainer: sawdoctor/shelfmark-torbox on GitHub. Do not post credentials or private library information in public issues.
